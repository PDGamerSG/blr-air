    Most air apps show the air right now. This agent looks ahead 48 hours, weighs pollution, rain and heat hour by hour, and gives you the best consecutive slot. If even the best slot is unhealthy, it says so.

    01 ✦ How Gaali reads the sky

    Four signals.
    One window.

    01 / 04

    US AQI

    The headline number, averaged across your window. The worst single hour is still reported, so one bad hour can't hide.

    + 1 × AQI

    02 / 04

    PM2.5

    AQI already counts particles. They get extra weight on purpose, so smoggy, particle-heavy hours sink in the ranking.

    + 0.5 × PM2.5

    03 / 04

    Rain

    Hourly chance of rain. You see the peak hour, not a soothing block average.

    + 1.5 × rain %

    04 / 04

    Heat

    Every degree above 30 °C costs five points. Bengaluru afternoons, we see you.

    + 5 × max(0, °C − 30)
    cost = AQI + 0.5·PM2.5 + 1.5·rain% + 5·max(0, °C−30)

    Lowest mean cost wins; ties go to the earlier hour. Only complete windows between 05:00 and 21:00 local count. This is a transparent preference heuristic, not a medical model.

      The fine print, unredacted
      
          
      
          
        
            
      • Modelled air, not station readings. Open-Meteo / CAMS forecasts, not CPCB monitors or street-level sensors.
        • 
            
      • Not medical advice. The best window can still be unhealthy. When it is, the agent says so and suggests going indoors.
        • 
            
      • Activity hours only. Windows run 05:00–21:00 local. That is a preference, not a sunrise calculation.
        • 
            
      • No invented data. An hour with missing rain, heat, AQI or PM2.5 is skipped, never assumed clean.
        • 
            
      • Privacy by design. Website plans live briefly in server memory for downloads. Prompts and audio are processed by the configured services; see the privacy details below.
        • 
            
      • Next 48 hours only. Today, tomorrow, morning, afternoon or evening. Exact clock times aren't supported.

        Privacy and terms of use
        
      BLR Air helps you choose an outdoor window from model forecasts. It is not medical advice, a venue booking or a guarantee of safe conditions. Recheck conditions and venue access before travelling.
      
        
      Your request is processed by this server and, when configured, ASI:One for language understanding. Place names or coordinates go to Open-Meteo for forecasts. Venue queries go to Google Places when requested. Uploaded recordings and spoken replies use Sarvam. Do not include sensitive personal information.
      
        
      Website plans are kept temporarily in server memory so you can download a calendar or spoken answer; they expire and are lost on restart. The application does not persist your recordings or customer-review content. Infrastructure and third-party services have their own data policies. Agent-to-agent usage counters store keyed sender hashes and aggregate counts.
      
        
      Google Maps venue data includes attributed ratings and a limited, relevance-ranked sample of customer reviews. Reviews are written by their authors and are not verified or endorsed by BLR Air. Google Maps content is subject to the Google Maps terms and Google Privacy Policy.
